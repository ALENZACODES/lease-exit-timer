01 / YOUR PLAN

Start with the notice.

Use the actual send day. Delivery time is added below.

Rent due on the 1st usually means the period ends on the last day of the month. Days 29 to 31 need care in shorter months.

Check your lease too. The rental period is set by your tenancy agreement, and the day you usually pay is not always proof of it, especially on the 29th, 30th or 31st.

The end date written in your lease. A one year lease that began on September 1, 2026 usually ends on August 31, 2027. If the term has already ended and you still live there, choose Monthly instead.

For example, a rental week paid on Monday ends on Sunday.

Only used to show what one late day would cost. It stays in this page.

How will it reach the landlord?

Hand it to the landlord or an authorized employee, or leave it where the landlord receives mail.

The Board's Consent to Service by Email form (v. 01/04/2022) lists rent increase notices, landlord entry notices, documents for a Board application and tenancy communication. It does not name a tenant's notice to end the tenancy, so check what your consent says.

Courier dates move past Saturdays and Sundays, which LTB rule 1.1 names as holidays, and past weekdays when the Board's offices may be closed: the Ontario holidays in the Legislation Act, 2006, s. 88, and Civic Holiday, which is not on that list. Rule 1.1 turns on actual office closures, and no Board closure calendar could be found, so those weekdays are marked UNKNOWN and counted as closed, and the result says when that changes the date. An unplanned closure cannot be known ahead, so leave a spare day.

03 / SHOW THE WORK
Not a date pulled out of a box.
01
Count delivery first
Mail counts on the fifth day after mailing. Hand delivery counts that day. Email needs the landlord's written consent. Courier has a separate rule.
RTA section 191
02
Count the notice
Usually 60 days for monthly or fixed term tenancies and 28 days for daily or weekly. Leave out the day notice is given. Count the termination day.
Legislation Act sections 88 and 89 (3)
03
Land on a period end
A monthly or weekly notice ends on a rental period end. A fixed term ends on its expiry. February and March have a small rule of their own.
RTA section 44

    
The rules, in plain view.
Special cases this tool does not date
A written agreement to end early, a refused assignment, an N12 or N13 notice, the standard lease demand path, care home tenancies and the process for tenants who have experienced violence can change the ordinary result. Read the official form and get advice for those paths.
This tool assumes the ordinary rental period follows the rent due day. If the agreement uses an unusual period or a rent day of 29, 30 or 31, confirm the period end in the agreement. A fixed term that is too late is shown with a possible ordinary monthly date after it, not treated as an early exit.
How courier and email connect to the Act
Courier and email timing come from the Board's Rules of Procedure, rules 3.1, 3.5 and 3.9. Those rules are written for documents in Board matters, and they may not settle every question about a notice given before any application. Mail timing comes from the Act itself, s. 191 (3). Under s. 191 (2) a notice that actually reached the landlord in time can still count, but that is a fallback, not a plan. Yearly tenancies are not offered here.
