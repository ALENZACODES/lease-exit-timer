## The problem
Ontario tenants often miss a valid notice day, or send an email that is not the official Board form. The ordinary rules are public: 60 days for most monthly and fixed-term tenancies, 28 days for daily or weekly, a termination date on the last day of a rental period or on the fixed expiry, and a short special rule for the last day of February or March.

## The idea
A static calculator: enter the day you can give notice and the kind of tenancy, get the earliest ordinary end date, the last day to give that notice, and a courtesy draft that points to Form N9. Label the work as not legal advice.

## Why this fits the Buildathon
The event wants a working core feature someone else can try, plus a short account of what was tested. A date tool with unit tests and cited sources fits a beginner-friendly, low-code brief without a paid API.

## Scope for this build
- Ordinary N9 notice paths only
- February and March special rule when it is needed
- Draft note, not a replica of the official form
- Exceptions listed, not dated

## Out of scope
Filing a notice, legal advice, or calculating every special path on the N9 instruction pages.

## User
A renter in Ontario who has a move-out date in mind and needs the last ordinary day to give notice.

## Success
A judge can enter today's date, see a termination date, open the cited N9 link, and copy a draft in one sitting.
