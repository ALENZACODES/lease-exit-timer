Lease Exit Timer

Enter the day you can give notice and the kind of tenancy you have. The page counts the earliest ordinary end date under Ontario’s public notice rules and drafts a short note you can paste while you fill official Form N9.

Built for Silicon Valley Buildathon

Your dates

If rent is due on the 1st, the rental period usually ends on the last day of the month.

The official N9 example says that if you pay each Monday, the termination date must be a Sunday.

Result

Enter a notice date and press Count the date.

Draft note

This is a plain-language note, not Form N9. Copy the official PDF, fill it, and give it to your landlord.


        
        

      

    

    

      
Rules this page uses

      

      

        
This page does not give legal advice. Other official paths can change the date, including a written agreement to end early, a refused assignment, an N12 or N13 from the landlord, care-home rules, or the Board’s process for tenants who have experienced sexual or domestic violence. Those paths are listed, not dated, because they need facts this form does not collect.
